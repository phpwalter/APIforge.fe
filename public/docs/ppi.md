## 1. Purpose of This Page

This page explains how you can ask APIForge not to sell or share your personal information where applicable privacy laws give you that right.

APIForge is an OpenAPI design, governance, and collaboration platform. Customers use APIForge to create, edit, validate, store, and manage API specifications, including OpenAPI files, API metadata, examples, schemas, security definitions, generated documentation, and APIForge-specific editor metadata such as `x-apiforge` fields.

This page is intended to provide a clear method for exercising privacy choices related to the sale or sharing of personal information. It should be read together with the APIForge Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Security Policy, Community Guidelines, and any applicable Data Processing Addendum.

---

## 2. Important Definitions

For purposes of this page:

**Personal Information** means information that identifies, relates to, describes, can reasonably be associated with, or could reasonably be linked to an individual or household.

**Sell** generally means disclosing personal information to another party for monetary or other valuable consideration, where applicable law defines such activity as a sale.

**Share** generally means disclosing personal information to another party for cross-context behavioral advertising or similar advertising-related purposes, where applicable law defines such activity as sharing.

**Cross-Context Behavioral Advertising** generally means targeting advertising to a person based on personal information obtained from that person's activity across different websites, applications, services, or businesses.

**Customer Content** means content that customers or authorized users upload, create, generate, store, transmit, or manage through APIForge. This may include OpenAPI files, schemas, examples, API descriptions, comments, internal notes, vendor extensions, project metadata, and related artifacts.

**APIForge Editor Metadata** means APIForge-specific authoring or workflow data stored with an OpenAPI file or project, including metadata contained in `x-apiforge` extension fields.

## 3. APIForge's Position on Selling Personal Information

APIForge does not intend to sell personal information in the ordinary meaning of selling customer data to data brokers or unrelated third parties.

However, privacy laws may define "sale" broadly. Certain disclosures, analytics activities, advertising technologies, integrations, or third-party service arrangements may be treated as a sale or sharing of personal information under some laws, depending on how they are configured and used.

For that reason, APIForge provides this opt-out page so eligible individuals can exercise their rights clearly and directly.

---

## 4. APIForge's Position on Sharing Personal Information

APIForge does not use Customer Content to create third-party behavioral advertising profiles.

APIForge does not intend to share OpenAPI files, API definitions, internal API documentation, security schemes, examples, customer schemas, or `x-apiforge` metadata for cross-context behavioral advertising.

APIForge may use limited website, device, cookie, or usage data for analytics, measurement, product improvement, security, fraud prevention, operational monitoring, or marketing attribution. Depending on applicable law and configuration, some advertising or analytics disclosures may be considered "sharing."

If you opt out, APIForge will process your request as described in this page.

An opt-out request may apply to personal information associated with you, including, where applicable:

To request deletion, access, correction, export, or account closure, use the privacy request process described in the APIForge Privacy Policy.

## 6. Customer Content and API Specifications

APIForge treats Customer Content differently from general website or marketing data.

Customer Content may include business-confidential API designs, internal service names, data models, examples, schemas, authentication definitions, endpoint structures, comments, governance notes, generated documentation, mock definitions, and APIForge editor metadata.

APIForge does not sell Customer Content.

APIForge does not share Customer Content for cross-context behavioral advertising.

APIForge may process Customer Content only as needed to provide, secure, maintain, troubleshoot, improve, support, or operate the APIForge service, or as otherwise described in the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Security Policy, Data Processing Addendum, or written agreement with the customer.

## 9. Verification

APIForge may need to verify your identity before processing certain privacy requests.

For a Do Not Sell or Share request, APIForge will generally request only the information reasonably necessary to process the request. If the request relates only to browser-based tracking choices, identity verification may not be required.

If your request involves account-level personal information, APIForge may verify your identity by asking you to:

- Confirm control of the email address associated with your account
- Sign in to your APIForge account
- Provide workspace or organization details
- Confirm limited account-related information
- Provide documentation of agent authority, if submitted by an authorized agent

APIForge will not ask for unnecessary sensitive information to process an opt-out request.

## 10. Browser-Based Opt-Outs and Global Privacy Control

Some privacy choices are connected to the browser, device, or cookie environment you use.

Where required by applicable law and supported by APIForge's systems, APIForge will honor recognized browser-based opt-out signals, such as Global Privacy Control, as a request to opt out of sale or sharing for the browser or device sending the signal.

Because browser-based signals may apply only to the browser or device from which they are sent, you may need to enable the signal separately on each browser and device you use.

If you clear cookies, use a different browser, use a different device, or access APIForge while logged out, your preferences may need to be reset or re-sent.

## 12. What Happens After You Opt Out

After APIForge processes your opt-out request, APIForge will take reasonable steps to stop selling or sharing your personal information as required by applicable law.

This may include:

- Updating your privacy preference record
- Suppressing advertising-related disclosures where applicable
- Disabling certain third-party advertising or marketing tags for your browser or account, where technically feasible
- Communicating the opt-out choice to relevant service providers, contractors, or advertising technology partners where required
- Retaining a record of the request to demonstrate compliance

APIForge may continue to disclose personal information to service providers, contractors, processors, subprocessors, security vendors, hosting providers, payment processors, analytics providers, customer support systems, and other vendors where such disclosure is permitted by law and necessary for legitimate business, operational, security, legal, or contractual purposes.

## 13. What an Opt-Out Request Does Not Do

A Do Not Sell or Share request does not automatically:

- Delete your APIForge account
- Delete your workspace
- Delete OpenAPI files
- Delete Customer Content
- Cancel your subscription
- Disable required security logging
- Stop transactional service emails
- Stop product-critical notifications
- Remove information from backup systems immediately
- Remove data that APIForge must retain for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, dispute-resolution, or contractual reasons
- Prevent disclosures to service providers or processors that are not considered sales or sharing under applicable law

If you want APIForge to delete, correct, access, or export your personal information, submit the appropriate privacy request under the APIForge Privacy Policy.
